3. WHAT THE APP DOES, AND FOR WHOM
Wecycle is a free campus marketplace and noticeboard. Members list items to
sell, lend or give away; post requests for things they need; report lost and
found property; post and RSVP to campus events; and post or find small paid
campus gigs.
Problem: each term students discard furniture and textbooks the next intake
buys new, and campus notices scatter across unsearchable WhatsApp groups.
Audience: students and staff at the campuses served; a university email is
required so members are verified classmates.
Value: free, no commission, no advertising. The app handles no money — it
introduces two people who meet in person and complete the exchange themselves.

4. ACCESSING THE MAIN FEATURES
Browse: opens on the marketplace; category chips and search filter it.
Listing: tap any card for photos, price, condition, location, poster, comments
and Contact.
Lost and found: "All lost" on home, or the bottom bar.
Events: calendar icon in the bottom bar; open one to RSVP.
Share card: the share icon on a listing or event makes a branded image and
offers the iOS share sheet.
Post something: + in the bottom bar. Adding a photo prompts for camera or
photo library permission.
Report or block: the ... menu on any listing, comment or profile.
Delete account: Menu > Settings > Delete account, or wecycle.page/delete-account

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase — database, authentication, image storage, auth email.
Vercel — hosts wecycle.page and the app's one server endpoint.
remove.bg — optional background removal, run only when a member taps that
control on a photo they are uploading.
Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity — product analytics.
DiceBear — generated placeholder avatar images.
No payment processor, no advertising network, no generative AI service. No
purchases, subscriptions or in-app payments exist anywhere in the app.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Every feature behaves identically in every region and storefront. Nothing
is geo-gated; the app requests no location permission and collects no device
location. Prices display in Indian rupees because the campuses served are in
India, but no payment is taken in the app in any currency. The university-email
sign-up rule is an eligibility rule applied identically everywhere, not a
regional difference.
